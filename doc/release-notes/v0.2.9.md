YetEase Music 0.2.9：修复歌曲列表里的歌手名点不动。

## 修复

- **歌曲列表里歌手名用鼠标点不动**（[#17](https://github.com/Gloridust/YetEaseMusic/issues/17)，感谢 [@jing2uo](https://github.com/jing2uo) 反馈）：现在单击歌手名会打开歌手页，看他的热门歌曲和专辑。点在名字之间的分隔符上不会误开，在名字上双击仍然是播放这首歌

## 升级

已经装了 0.2.8 的话，不用再来这里下载：应用启动后会提示这个版本，点「立即更新」即可（deb 会弹出系统授权框）；也可以在 *设置 → 关于* 里手动「检查更新」。

YetEase Music 0.2.9 fixes artist names in song lists not responding to mouse clicks (#17, thanks @jing2uo): clicking an artist now opens the artist page, clicks on the separators are ignored, and double-clicking still plays the song. If you are on 0.2.8, the app will offer this update itself – just click "Update now", or check manually under Settings → About.
