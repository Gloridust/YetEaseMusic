YetEase Music 0.2.6：大字号与高分屏适配，侧栏可调宽。

本版的界面改进大部分来自 [@vickwv](https://github.com/vickwv) 的贡献（[#7](https://github.com/Gloridust/YetEaseMusic/pull/7)、[#8](https://github.com/Gloridust/YetEaseMusic/pull/8)），感谢！

## 改进

- **系统文字大小跟随整体界面缩放**：调大系统的「文字大小」后，侧栏、顶栏、播放栏等会和文字一起等比放大，不再出现文字被裁切、导航折行。窗口放不下时会自动少放大一些，或切到紧凑布局
- **侧栏可拖动调宽**（200–420），双击拖动条恢复默认，宽度会记住
- 侧栏 logo 自适应宽度，导航文字超长时单行省略
- 没在播放时，播放栏左侧显示「未在播放」占位，不再一片空白
- 提示条显示在播放栏上方，不再挡住播放按钮
- 心动模式换成心形加心电图的图标，更好认
- Linux：窗口外加一圈细描边，贴着其他白色窗口时不会糊在一起；最大化或全屏时自动去掉

---

YetEase Music 0.2.6 makes the system text size scale the whole interface, so larger text no longer gets clipped; when the window is too small it scales a little less or switches to the compact layout. The sidebar can now be resized by dragging (double-click to reset) and remembers its width, the player bar shows a placeholder when idle, notifications no longer cover the playback controls, the heartbeat mode gets a clearer icon, and on Linux the window has a thin outline that disappears when maximized. Most of these improvements were contributed by @vickwv in #7 and #8 – thank you!
